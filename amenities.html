Nearby Amenities in Mountain's Edge, Las Vegas

Dining, parks, grocery, healthcare, and schools around southwest Las Vegas 89178—on the map and in plain language.

Interactive amenity map

Filter by category to see places near the center of Mountain's Edge (36.0051°N, 115.2552°W). Without a Maps API key, the page shows an embed map and verified curated places.

Dining and cafes

Mountain's Edge sits along the Blue Diamond Rd corridor with quick access to casual restaurants and coffee in the marketplace area. Many households also head toward Rhodes Ranch, Silverado Ranch, or resort corridors for broader dining choices on weekends.

Parks and recreation

Exploration Peak Park (9700 S Buffalo Dr) offers desert trails and views at the edge of the area. Mountain's Edge Regional Park (8101 W Mountains Edge Pkwy) is a Clark County park with reservable pavilions. Neighborhood parks such as Paiute Park (9347 S Cimarron Rd) and Nathaniel Jones Park (8800 Sparkling Chandon Dr) provide fields and recreation within the master plan.

Golf

Rhodes Ranch Golf Club (20 E Rhodes Ranch Pkwy) is a public course northwest of Mountain's Edge. Tee times and leagues are popular with southwest Las Vegas residents who want golf without driving to the Strip resorts.

Grocery and everyday shopping

Albertsons (7975 Blue Diamond Rd) at Mountain's Edge Marketplace covers weekly grocery runs. The in-store Albertsons Pharmacy at the same address handles many prescription needs. Additional retailers along Blue Diamond Rd extend shopping toward the 215 belt.

Healthcare

Southern Hills Hospital & Medical Center (9300 W Sunset Rd, Las Vegas) is a full-service hospital used by many Mountain's Edge households. Urgent care and physician offices also cluster along Blue Diamond Rd and toward the 215 belt corridor—confirm networks with your insurance when you relocate.

Schools

Mountain's Edge is served by Clark County School District schools. William V. Wright Elementary School (8425 Bob Fisk Ave) and Wilbur & Theresa Faiss Middle School (9525 W Maule Ave) are examples buyers research in 89178. Which CCSD schools are assigned to your Mountain's Edge address? Verify with the CCSD Zoning Search before you buy.

Commute and regional access (approximate drive times)

Drive times vary with traffic; these are typical mid-day or off-peak estimates from central Mountain's Edge:

  • Las Vegas Strip (central resorts): about 20–35 minutes
  • Harry Reid International Airport: about 20–30 minutes
  • Downtown Summerlin: about 25–40 minutes via the 215 belt and Charleston Blvd corridors
  • Downtown Las Vegas: about 25–35 minutes

Many commuters use Buffalo Dr, Blue Diamond Rd, and the 215 belt to reach employment centers across the valley.
